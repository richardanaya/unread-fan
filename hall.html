The great hall

West is the ladies' rooms. East is the storehouse. North is the inner garden. South is the engawa.

No one else is here. A sensu lies on the boards near the middle. The south doors stand open on the purple evening. North, through the far doors, is the inner garden. South is the engawa. West is the ladies' rooms. East is the storehouse. Stay on the wood; the open sides drop away.
