The shrine path

West is the forest. East is the courtyard. South, on the near path, is the market. North is the shrine hall.

Tamayori stands at the foot of the steps and looks down the path, toward the forest. Gyōban stands nearer, on the dirt, and looks toward her. The pond blocks the east side until you come down onto the near path. The market is on the near edge. North, through the shrine, is the hall.
