The cedar path

East returns to the shrine. North, at the far end of the path, the stone steps climb the ridge. West is the rice fields.

Minamoto no Haru stands on the path and looks east, back toward the shrine. A magatama lies on the near dirt. The trees close in farther up the path, so the prince stays on the dirt. Stone steps wait at the far end of the path. West, the dirt opens into the rice fields.
